My Squishy Lab Privacy

My Squishy Lab is a virtual toy workshop. The game stores your creations, game snapshots, settings, rewards and progress on your device.

Playing and saving

The game has no player account, chat, advertising or payment feature. Friends are fictional characters. A gift in the game copies your toy for that character and keeps the original on your shelf.

Backups and deletion

You can export and import a backup in Grown-ups. The exported file can contain your creations and in-game images. You choose where to keep it or share it. Removing game data from your device does not remove backup copies you exported.

Browser edition

The browser edition loads game files from GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process standard request information, such as your IP address, to serve the files. Game progress stays in your browser. GitHub privacy information

Contact

For a question about the game or your data, use the developer contact shown on the app store listing.
