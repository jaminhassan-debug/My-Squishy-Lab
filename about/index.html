Why we made the game

A place to turn little ideas into squishies.

A squishy can begin with the simplest idea. A favourite colour, a funny shape, a little sparkle or a dumpling with a personality of its own.

We wanted to give those ideas a workshop. Somewhere players could choose the details, watch their mixture fill a mould and meet the toy they had imagined.

That is the heart of My Squishy Lab. The making matters as much as the finished toy, from the first colour you stir to the first squeeze after the reveal.

Meet Jaida and Eeva

The people behind our little world.

Jaida and Eeva inspired the game and are its founders. Bringing them into My Squishy Lab as characters gives that beginning a place in the world they helped inspire.

You can meet them on the home screen and hear their helper tips when voice is enabled and a device voice is available. Their illustrated characters welcome players into a workshop built around making, experimenting and playing.

We have kept their real names at the centre of that story. When you see Jaida and Eeva holding their squishies, you are meeting the inspiration behind the lab.

A workshop you can make your own.

The game lets you decide what comes next. Choose a mould, blend colours, try a feel and add the little pieces that make your creation recognisable.

You might fill a dumpling with fruit slices, make a star with gems inside, or keep things simple and explore how different materials recover after a squeeze. Each choice becomes part of your toy.

Saving your favourites gives them a place on your shelf. Decorating your room and choosing a squishy to display carries the same idea into the workshop around you.

Keep creating with us.

My Squishy Lab continues to grow from the idea that started it. Jaida and Eeva are part of its identity, and every new creation adds another little story to your own collection.

Explore the making guides on our blog when you want an idea for your next toy, or head straight into the game and see where your imagination takes you.
