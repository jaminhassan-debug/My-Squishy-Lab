A few things to know

Where are my squishies saved?

Your toys and progress are stored on the device and browser you use. Export a backup from Grown-ups before clearing your browser data or switching devices.

Can I play on a tablet?

Yes. Open the browser game and rotate your tablet to landscape. The website and blog can be read in either orientation.

Does the browser edition work offline?

The browser edition needs an internet connection to load the game files. Offline play is part of the planned dedicated mobile edition.
