Dumpling Squishy Game: Design Your Own Virtual Bao

My Squishy Lab · 19 November 2026

Make a virtual dumpling squishy in My Squishy Lab. Explore bao mould sizes, colour ideas, fillings and ways to build a dumpling collection.

Make your own dumpling squishy

A dumpling squishy combines a rounded body with the folded shape of a bao. In My Squishy Lab, you can turn that familiar outline into a virtual toy with your own colours, feel and inclusions.

Start from the workshop and choose a dumpling mould when it is available in your tray. The game includes several dumpling sizes, so you can build a small family of toys around the same shape.

Choose a size before adding details

The dumpling sizes run from Small through Medium and Large to Super Large. Look at the available mould choices and choose the one that suits the collection you want to make.

If you cannot see the dumpling you want, move through the mould pages. The simple tray shows fewer choices at once, while Grown-ups includes an expanded tray preference.

Keeping the size consistent is useful when you want to compare colours or fillings. Changing the size is more interesting when your aim is to create a matching set with a little variety.

Plan a colour that suits the rounded shape

A simple colour makes it easier to notice the dumpling's outline and folds. Try starting with pink, blue or violet, then reveal the toy before deciding which direction to take your next version.

For a set of dumplings, choose one colour family as the theme. Two neighbouring shades and one contrasting colour can give you a collection that feels connected without making every toy identical.

Think about the filling while choosing the body colour. A busy colour mixture and several inclusion types can compete for attention, while a simpler body gives fruit, pearls or gems more room to stand out.

Try three dumpling design ideas

For a fruit themed dumpling, choose one fruit slice type and a body colour that makes those slices visible. Strawberry, kiwi and orange are separate choices inside the fruit family.

For a sparkle themed dumpling, start with foil stars and inspect the result before adding another ingredient to your next recipe. Foil hearts become available through play or the Free Play setting.

For a gem themed dumpling, compare the round and diamond gem shapes in separate toys. Keeping the mould and colour the same makes the gem shape the main difference between the pair.

Choose how your dumpling recovers

The material choice changes the way your virtual dumpling responds. Soft Foam rises slowly after pressure, Dense Gel gives a softer wobble, and Bouncy Jelly returns with a quicker spring.

Press and release the samples in the material scene before choosing. Watch what happens after release, because the recovery is often the clearest difference between two materials.

Try building three dumplings with the same colour and filling but different feels. You will have a small comparison set that is useful whenever you want to choose a material for another design.

Pour into the mould and reveal

Once the recipe is ready, move to the pour scene and tap Pour. You can pause the pour, then continue until the mould is full and the mixture has settled.

Use Reveal when it becomes available. The making flow lets you see the same recipe move from your mixture into the mould and then into the finished toy.

Play with the finished dumpling

Begin with a small poke, release and watch the body recover. Then try the hand squeeze to see a broader change in its shape.

On a touch screen, use two fingers to stretch it and release both before starting again. The View controls let you inspect your dumpling from other angles when you want a closer look at its body and contents.

Build a bao collection

Save each finished dumpling so you can return to it from your shelf. A collection based on a small design rule, such as one fruit per colour, gives you a clear idea for the next toy.

When decorating is available, try displaying a favourite dumpling in Bao Bakery. The room and toy can share a theme, while the other saved versions stay in your local collection for another play session.
