Make your first squishy

My Squishy Lab · 19 November 2026

A guide to choosing your mould, mixing colours, adding fillings and revealing your first virtual squishy.

Start in the workshop

Open My Squishy Lab and tap Play, then Make. Turn your phone or tablet sideways so there is room for the making tools and your toy.

Choose a mould and a colour

Pick a shape for your squishy, then choose your colours and stir the mixture. Try a simple combination first so you can see how the colours blend.

Give your toy its own feel

Choose a feel for the mixture, then add the inclusions you like. Fruit slices, gems and glitter give you different ideas to explore. The choices become part of your squishy recipe.

Pour and reveal

Tap Pour and watch the mould fill. You can pause the pour. When the mixture has settled and Reveal becomes available, continue to meet your new squishy.

Try it out and save it

Poke the toy, try the hand squeeze, or use two fingers to stretch it on a touch screen. Finish and save your creation so you can find it on your shelf. Your progress is stored on this device and browser.
