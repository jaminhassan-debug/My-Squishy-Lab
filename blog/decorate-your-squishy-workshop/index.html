Workshop ideas

Make a workshop that feels like yours

My Squishy Lab · 19 November 2026

Choose a room, arrange decorations and give your favourite squishy a place on display.

Start with a room

When Decorate becomes available in your workshop, explore the room choices. Bao Bakery, Mint Greenhouse, Stargazing Loft and Seaside Studio each give the game a different setting.

Preview before choosing

Tap a decoration to preview it in the room. Confirm your choice to equip it. Decorations bought with earned Sparkle Coins show their cost before you confirm, and the balance is shown in Decorate.

Build around one favourite

Try a cushion and rug that suit your favourite squishy, then add a plant or wall picture. You can return to your room later and try another combination.

Carry the setting into play

Your equipped room background follows you through the making and squishing scenes, plus your shelf and Friends. Room previews stay in Decorate until you confirm them.

Give a creation a home

Use the display control in Decorate to choose a saved squishy for the selected room. You can keep making new toys and change the one on display whenever you like.
