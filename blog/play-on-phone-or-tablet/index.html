Getting started

Play on your phone or tablet

My Squishy Lab · 19 November 2026

Open the free browser game, add it to your home screen and keep your saved squishies backed up.

Play straight from the website

Visit mysquishylab.app and choose Play free. The browser game opens without a player account. Choose portrait or landscape to suit how you want to play.

Add a home screen shortcut

On iPhone or iPad, open the game in Safari and use Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open the game in Chrome and look in the browser menu for Add to Home screen or Install app. The exact wording depends on your browser.

Keep your progress safe

Creations and settings are saved in the browser on the device you use. Clearing that browser's website data can remove your progress. Before changing devices or clearing storage, open Grown-ups and export a backup.

Move a backup carefully

Keep the exported file somewhere you can find it. Open the game on the destination device and use the import option in Grown-ups to review and restore the backup. The game does not automatically sync progress between devices.

Mobile app editions

Dedicated Android, iPhone, iPad and Fire tablet editions are being prepared. Official store download links will be added to the download page when those editions are available. You can play the browser edition today.
